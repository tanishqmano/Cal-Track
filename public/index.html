Timezone
Used to guess which meal something belongs to when you don't say. It travels with the log, so the Claude connector uses it too — that server runs on UTC and has no clock of its own.
Daily targets
Targets travel with the log, so every device sees the same ones, and the assistant is told about them on every message.
Protein counts only from complete sources (whey, milk, egg, chicken, turkey, beef, fish, cheese, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, paneer, curd). Everything else is logged at 0 protein — calories, fat and carbs still count. Micronutrients are the exception: they count from every food, plants included. The default calories and macros are your own goals and add up to each other; the micronutrient defaults are the US RDAs for an adult man. They used to be the higher Indian ICMR-NIN numbers, which exist to offset the phytate in a rice-and-dal diet; if you go back to cooking that way, raise zinc, iron and magnesium again. Vitamin C is logged after cooking loss — a boiled or simmered vegetable keeps under half of what it started with, so soup, chilli and sambar count for far less than the raw weight suggests.
